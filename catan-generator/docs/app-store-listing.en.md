## Name

Hex Settlement Coach

## Subtitle

Board setup & placement coach

## Description

Hex Settlement Coach helps you set up fair hex settlement boards and choose strong opening positions.

Generate a classic island or a larger 5–6 player layout, then walk through the draft: ranked settlements, opening roads, harbor plans, and midgame tips for longest road, cities, and the robber.

Import a photo of a physical board, correct tiles in the grid, and confirm harbor placement before you simulate.

**Free:** unlimited standard board generation.  
**Premium:** Bonanza boards, placement simulation, and midgame advice.

Languages: English (default) and Norwegian.

This app is a coaching companion for the physical board game — not a full digital multiplayer game.
