Privacy Policy

Hex Settlement Coach · Last updated: 2026-08-09

Hex Settlement Coach (“the App”) helps you generate hex settlement boards and get setup advice. This policy describes what data we collect.

Data we store on your device

The App may store board sessions, language preference, and local Premium / trial status on your device (for example in browser storage or app preferences). This data does not leave your device unless you later enable cloud sync or analytics in a future update.

Photo import

If you use photo board import, images are processed on your device for recognition. We do not upload your photos to our servers in v1.0.

Purchases

When subscriptions are enabled, Apple processes payments. Purchase status may be validated through Apple / RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Crash and feedback (future)

Optional crash reporting or in-app feedback may send technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, error stacks) to help us fix bugs. You will be informed in the App Store privacy labels when this is active.

Contact

Questions: support@hexsettlement.coach

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
